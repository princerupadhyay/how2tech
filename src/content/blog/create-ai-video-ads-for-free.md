## How Far Do the Free Credits Go

First thing, the credits. This is the part nobody explains properly. On the free plan the credits can only be used for the **Veo 3.1** models, and those come in three types, **Lite**, **Fast** and **Quality**. Google lists Lite at 10 credits for one generation, Fast at 20 credits and Quality at 100 credits. So if you do the maths, **50 free credits per day** is five Lite clips or two Fast clips, and Quality you can't even do because it costs more than your whole day. Also the daily refresh starts when you make your first generation, so if you start today at 10 in the morning your next 50 credits come tomorrow after you generate again, and the credits you did not use don't roll over.

## Step 1: Open Google Flow and Start a Project

Step 1 is to open Google Flow and click on New project. That is it.

_Open Flow and click New project._

I didn't change anything on that home page and all that, I just went straight to the new project button.

## Step 2: Make a Reference Sheet

Step 2 is the **reference sheet** and this one is really the secret of the whole thing. So what I did is I took two photos of the product, a perfume bottle in my case, and I gave them to ChatGPT with this prompt, make me a real and consistent reference sheet for this perfume, make sure all angles are accurate and consistent. It worked for 1 minute and 4 seconds and gave me one image with the front view and the three quarter view and the sides and the back and the top, plus a close up of the cap and a close up of the label.

![ChatGPT turning two product photos into a multi-angle reference sheet](/images/create-ai-video-ads-for-free/03-make-reference-sheet.webp)
_Step 2: one reference sheet keeps the bottle consistent. Product shown: IQBAL by Scents N Stories, used for demonstration. Brand, product name and images belong to their respective owners._

Honestly any image tool can do this, I just happened to use ChatGPT, and by the way Flow also has its own image model called Nano Banana even on the free plan, so you can try it over there but I did it outside.

## Step 3: Write the Shot List

Step 3 is the **shot list**. I opened a chat with Claude, gave it the reference sheet and the description of the perfume, and asked it to plan a commercial. It came back with a **30 to 40 second ad** broken into acts, a hook first and then the top notes and the heart notes and so on, and each act had a table with the shot number and what is in the shot and the camera movement and how many seconds it should be.

![AI-written shot list table for a 30 to 40 second perfume ad](/images/create-ai-video-ads-for-free/04-write-shot-list.webp)
_Step 3: let the AI plan the shots. Product shown: IQBAL by Scents N Stories, used for demonstration. Brand, product name and images belong to their respective owners._

I did not write any of that myself and I did not need to. You can use any AI chat for this step, okay?

## Step 4: Turn the Shot List into Prompts

Step 4 is where you turn that shot list into **video prompts**. I asked it to write a prompt for each generation and the thing I really liked is that it told me which ones need the reference sheet attached, it even said attach your reference sheet to every generation marked so the bottle stays accurate.

![AI-written video prompts with a note to attach the reference sheet](/images/create-ai-video-ads-for-free/05-generate-flow-prompts.webp)
_Step 4: one prompt per generation. Product shown: IQBAL by Scents N Stories, used for demonstration. Brand, product name and images belong to their respective owners._

So the prompts start with 16:9 cinematic luxury fragrance commercial and then the shots, and you just copy one prompt at a time.

## Step 5: Set Up the Agent Settings

Step 5 is the settings inside Flow, and do not skip this one. Open the agent settings and you will see **confirm before generating**. I kept it on always so Flow asks me before it spends any credits, because with 50 a day you can burn through your credits really fast if you let it generate on its own. Then for video I picked **Veo 3.1 Lite**, because it is the cheapest one at 10 credits, and for the shape I left it on **16:9** and you can go **9:16** if your ad is for Reels or Shorts.

![Google Flow agent settings showing Always confirm and Veo 3.1 Lite](/images/create-ai-video-ads-for-free/06-set-agent-settings.webp)
_Step 5: confirm before spending credits and pick Lite._

## The Logic Behind the Reference Sheet

Now let me explain the logic behind this. Why do we make a **reference sheet** first and attach it again and again? Because every clip is a separate generation and the AI does not remember what your bottle looked like in the last clip. So if you only type the bottle in words then in one clip the cap will be gold and in the next clip it will be something else, you know, and that is exactly what makes an AI ad look fake. When you attach the same picture every time the bottle stays more or less the same, not always, but a lot better. And the **shot list** is for the same reason. If you generate random clips then you get random clips, but when you plan the hook first and then the middle and then the end then you can actually put them together as one ad.
