## What Does **Clipping** Pay?

Now that I have made sure that you know why this is the right time, let me first tell you about the **money part** because a lot of videos on YouTube skip this. **Clipping** means you take a short piece, usually **15 to 60 seconds**, out of somebody else's long stream and post it on your own account. Some streamers and brands run **clipping campaigns** where they pay you for every **1,000 views** your clip gets. The number I found is about **$1 per 1,000 views**, that is what the typical campaign lists on **Content Rewards** which is one of the biggest clipping boards, with a **$200 cap per clip** and **10 percent fee** taken from your payout. And these are the listed rates, not what you actually take home, so a new clip can earn very little. So this is not a **get rich quick** thing, it is more like a **side income** which grows when you post a lot of clips and so on. I am telling you this at the start so that you don't think one clip is going to pay your rent.

## Find a **Stream** and Check **You Are Allowed to Clip It**

Now the first thing is the **stream itself**. Go to **YouTube** and search for **GTA 6 stream** and then tap on the **Live filter** or just pick one of the long streams which are already finished.

_Find a GTA 6 stream, and check the streamer allows clipping_

Over here comes the part which most people skip. You are clipping somebody else's stream so you have to check if that streamer is okay with it. Just **Google it**, something like **does this streamer allow clipping**, and then see if they have a **clipping campaign** or they have said anything about clippers. I did the same thing in my video before I started. And by the way the tool I am going to show you also asks you to tick a box that says **you have the right to use the video**, so it is not just me being careful, the tool itself is asking. Also stay away from **leaked footage** and **story spoilers** because **Rockstar** removes those, and a clip of the streamer reacting is a lot safer than a clip of a raw cutscene. And one more thing which most people don't tell you. **Rockstar's own policy** says it is happy for fans to post gameplay, but for **non-commercial use**, and it says non-commercial means you don't make money from the footage. **Take-Two** can also take content down any time. So if you earn from **GTA 6 clips** then it depends on them tolerating it, and I am not a lawyer so read that policy page yourself.

## **Source:** Paste the Link and Find the Moments

Once you have the stream copy the link. Then open **Wanted Clips by ClipOnIt** in your browser, and I did this on a laptop because it was easier for me to record my screen but the same steps can be done on **your phone** also. On the first page there is a box where you **paste a link**.

![Wanted Clips by ClipOnIt with a YouTube stream link pasted into the link box](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-01-paste-stream-link.webp)
_Paste the stream link and press Check the link_

Paste your **YouTube link** over there and press **Check the link**. After that it shows you the **duration** of the video and there is that **checkbox** I was telling you about.

![Tick the box saying you have the right to use the video, then press Find my moments](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-03-confirm-rights-find-my-moments.webp)
_Confirm you have the right to use the video, then find the moments_

Tick it and press **Find my moments**. The stream I tried was **2 hours 5 minutes 45 seconds** long and the tool has to go through the whole thing, so just wait and don't keep pressing buttons.

## **Moments:** Pick What to Clip

Then it takes you to **step 2** and the whole job is just **four steps**: **Source**, **Moments**, **Adjust** and **Export**.

![The four-step bar in Wanted Clips: Source, Moments, Adjust, Export](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-04-four-step-workflow.webp)
_The whole job is four steps_

For my stream it came back with **29 suggested moments**, and each one has an **AI score** next to it. The top ones were **83**, **82** and **78**.

![Suggested clip moments with AI scores and Keep and Adjust buttons](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-05-suggested-moments-ai-score.webp)
_The tool suggests moments and scores them, you decide_

Now this score is just the tool's guess and I would not blindly trust it, because honestly the funniest part of a stream is not always the highest score. So watch the **preview** and then press **Keep** on the ones you like, and press **Adjust** on the one you want to work on first. There is also an **Ignore** button for the ones you don't want.

## **Adjust:** Make It Vertical, Add Captions, Trim

**Step 3** is where the clip actually becomes **vertical**. In the **Adjust** page there is a **Short Layout** section and I used the **Facecam plus game** layout which puts the streamer's face on top and the gameplay at the bottom, because that is what you see on most **gaming Shorts**.

![Short layout options in the Adjust step with Facecam plus game highlighted](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-06-short-layout-facecam-game.webp)
_Choose a vertical layout_

There are other layouts also like **Full-screen game** and **Big facecam** so you can try them and see what looks better. Then go to the **Captions** tab and pick a style.

![Caption style choices with Captions tab and Outline style marked](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-07-caption-styles.webp)
_Pick a caption style_

I picked **Outline**, but there are a lot of others like **Karaoke**, **Box** and **Glow**, so it is kind of a personal choice. **Captions matter** a lot because most people watch shorts with the **sound off**, so if you have no captions then they just scroll past you.

After that go to the **Cut** tab. You can move the **start** and the **end** of the clip by **1 second** at a time, and there is a **Cut here** button which removes a bad bit in the middle.

![Cut tab with start and end trim controls and the Prepare the MP4 button marked](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-08-cut-trim-prepare-mp4.webp)
_Trim the clip, then press Prepare the MP4_

My clip was **21 seconds** long, so keep yours short like that and make it start right on the moment and not before it. Then press **Prepare the MP4**. Don't start with a **slow intro**, start right on the **funny part**, okay? That is the **single biggest thing** for a short.

## **Export:** Download and Post

**Step 4** is the **export page**. It says **Mission Accomplished** and your clip is ready.

![Export page showing 1080 by 1920 vertical clip with no watermark and the Download the MP4 button](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-09-download-mp4-1080x1920.webp)
_The export page: your MP4 is ready_

The file I got was **1080 by 1920**, which is **vertical 9:16**, and the watermark option said **No**. The page also says that exporting does not use up your quota. So just press **Download the MP4** and it is on your phone or laptop. If you scroll down there is a **Post** section where you can connect **TikTok** and **YouTube Shorts**.

![Post section with TikTok and YouTube Shorts connect buttons and Instagram Reels marked coming soon](/images/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams/make-money-clipping-gta-6-streams-10-connect-tiktok-youtube-shorts.webp)
_Connect TikTok or YouTube Shorts, or download and upload yourself_

**Instagram Reels** was showing **coming soon** when I recorded this, so for Reels you just download the MP4 and upload it yourself in the Instagram app. One more small thing, the page says the **first half second** of the video is used as the **cover** on TikTok and YouTube, so pick a **good first frame** before you post.
