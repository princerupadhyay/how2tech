## Why GTA 6 Features Matter in Launch Week

So since GTA 6 is launching this Thursday, 19 November 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X and S so the whole internet is going to be talking about it for the next few weeks. And a lot of those posts are mixing leaks and rumours and real announcements together and honestly you can't tell which is which anymore. Reviews is also reportedly coming on 18 November, the day before launch, but over here I want to be clear that this date is reported and Rockstar has not confirmed it. Okay?

In addition, pre-loading is already open for the ones who pre-ordered the digital version. The reason is simple, everybody wants to know if the wait was worth it and nobody can say yet. Nobody outside Rockstar and the reviewers has played the final game, so everything in this particular article comes from Rockstar's own previews and interviews, mainly the Game Informer cover story from September and the IGN interview which came out in October. I will tell you where each thing comes from and I will also tell you what is still unproven. Because a feature on paper is one thing and a feature in your hands is another thing, you know?

## 1. The Map: Leonida Is Twice the Size of GTA 5

Number one is the map. Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Game Informer that **Leonida** is the biggest map they have ever built and roughly twice the size of the **GTA 5 map**, and that is the confirmed part. You will also see other websites throwing around different comparisons with older games and I am not going to repeat those, I am only taking the number which Rockstar itself gave. What is unproven is simple, big maps are really easy to build and really hard to fill. A lot of empty space is a lot of empty space, and all that.

## 2. Six Regions Beyond Vice City

Number two is the six regions beyond **Vice City**. Everybody knows Vice City but Leonida is the whole state and Rockstar has put names on six main areas in its screenshots, that is Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Mount Kalaga National Park, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Grassrivers. Grassrivers is the swampy one and Ambrosia is the rural and industrial one with a biker gang in it. Seen in previews, not played. So the question is not how many regions there are, the question is do they really feel different when you are driving through them at 2 in the morning.

## 3. Hurricanes and a New Weather System

Number three is the **weather system**. This one is from the Game Informer cover story and it says hurricanes and tropical storms can hit Leonida, and wind is also there so it moves the grass in the swamps and ripples the water and stirs up debris on rooftops. Rockstar has not said how often the storms happen and it has not said if they play any part in the story missions. So this particular feature is confirmed but how much it matters is not.

## 4. NPCs That React to You

Number four is the **NPCs**, the normal people walking around. From the IGN interview, if you keep following someone or you stand there listening to their conversation for too long then they can confront you or attack you or just try to run away. Some civilians are armed too and they can start the trouble themselves, and the characters come in different heights and body shapes. I really like this idea, but what is unproven is whether it stays this interesting on the fortieth hour or if you just stop noticing it.

## 5. Over 170 Animal Species

Number five is the wildlife and this one is a big one. Rockstar confirmed more than **170 animal species**, and that is more than 60 types of birds and more than 30 land mammals and then fish and reptiles and so on. Manatees, flamingos, pelicans and alligators are the ones which Rockstar named. Some animals can be tracked as Rare or Discoverable or Legendary, but Rockstar has not told which animal goes in which group. So what you actually do with all those animals is still unproven.

## 6. A Smarter Police and Wanted System

Number six is the **police and wanted system**. In the IGN interview the wanted stars show as hollow outlines when the officers don't know what you look like, so it is not just about how many stars you have anymore, it is also about whether they know your face. Aiming a weapon at someone can also be enough to make them run. People who watched the Extended Look from August also say the six star wanted level is back, but that one I am only taking from the coverage of the Extended Look so treat it as seen and not as officially explained. The unproven part is how the police behave when 10 things are going wrong at the same time.

## 7. Jason and Lucia as Dual Protagonists

Number seven is the two main characters, **Jason and Lucia**. You can play both and you can switch between them. Your partner can drive while you listen to the radio and in a chase you can shoot from the passenger seat, that is what the IGN interview says. It is kind of like when you are playing a co-op game and you give the controller to your friend for the driving part and, anyway, the unproven part is whether the switching feels natural in the middle of a mission or just gets in the way.

## 8. Robberies With Real Choices

Number eight is the **robberies** and I think this is the most interesting design idea of all ten. In the IGN interview, blasting a safe open is faster but it can damage whatever is inside and finding the combination takes a lot more time but you keep more of the loot. The police can also wait outside the place, so some buildings have another exit for you. Speed versus payout is a real choice, and it is confirmed. Whether every robbery gives you that choice or only the first few, that is unproven.

## 9. Side Activities Across Leonida

Number nine is the **side activities**. From the Game Informer interview, you will have kayaking, scuba diving, base jumping, jet skiing, zoo visits, street racing, off-road racing, wrestling, pool, mini-golf and gyms and more. Seen and confirmed in previews. Side activities are always there in a big open world game and the real question is do they have a reason to exist or are they just there to fill the map.

## 10. The Small Details That Make Leonida Feel Lived In

Number ten is the small **living world details**. Your phone appears physically in the character's hand instead of a separate overlay. Your partner can text you normal everyday messages. Safehouses have routines and visitors, and the IGN interview says there are more radio and TV ads than any earlier GTA. None of these small things will sell the game alone, and all of them together can make a place feel lived in, which is the whole point.

## What Is Not Confirmed Yet

Now there are things which are not confirmed and I want to be fair about it. As far as I know there is no online mode announced for launch day, there is no PC version announced and there is no cross-play announced. If you read somewhere that these are confirmed then check the source, because a lot of the websites mix this up.

## Could It Really Be Rockstar's Biggest Game Ever?

Now let me explain the logic behind this. Why do I say these ten features could make **GTA 6** the biggest **Rockstar Games** game ever and why do I keep saying could? Because the map and the regions and the wildlife are the kind of things you can count, and Rockstar has told us the numbers. But NPC behaviour and police and robberies and all that are the kind of things you only know after you play, so a preview can't prove them. Rockstar also said in its announcement that the extra months are for polish, which is what you would expect, and polish is also not something you can see in an interview. And the previews are the moments which Rockstar picked to show us, okay? So for biggest ever you need both, the scale and the depth, and that is exactly what reviews and real players will decide in the next few days. Not me and not any list like this one.
