## How to **Get Paid in USD** in India

Now the money part, because this is where a lot of people get stuck, how is the dollar going to reach you over here in India. There are basically three ways that I came across, first one is **Payoneer**, second one is **PayPal** and third one is **Tipalti**. OneForma says Payoneer is the one they recommend and there is no payment cap on it. PayPal is allowed up to 300 dollars in a year and after that they want you to use Tipalti, so if you start with PayPal keep this limit in mind. Studypool and Braintrust have their own payout methods and they can change, so open the payment settings of the platform and check it before you start working. And by the way, whatever you earn from abroad is still income and it is **taxable in India**, so please talk to a chartered accountant and do not ignore it.

## **Scam Red Flags** for Remote Jobs

One more thing about safety. Never pay anyone to join any of these platforms. Braintrust says zero fees for talent and Appen says it never asks for money to apply or to start a job. If someone is messaging you on WhatsApp or Telegram and saying they can get you a job in these platforms for a fee, that is a scam, simple as that. Apply only from the official website.
