## GTA 6 Release Date and Time

First the **release date and time**. **GTA 6** launches on **Thursday 19 November 2026** at **midnight local time**. Local time, so it is not one big worldwide moment, it rolls with your time zone. For us in India that means **12 am IST** on 19 November. If you preloaded it then it just unlocks at that time, because **preloading** does not give you early access, it only saves you the download wait. ## GTA 6 Download Size and Storage

Now the **download size** and this one is a big one. **Rockstar Games** says you need up to **212 GB** of free space, depending on your device. So if your console has just 250 GB of storage left then it is going to be really tight and you should keep some extra room for the updates as well. Two small updates already came before the launch, one on 13 November and one on 16 November, and more will come I am quite sure. I have not seen any official word about a separate final patch on launch day itself. So finish your download and then check for updates once more before you start, you know.

## GTA 6 Platforms and PC Release

Now the **platforms** and this is where a lot of people get disappointed. **GTA 6** is on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X/S**. That is it. It is not on the **PS4** or the **Xbox One**, and there is no sign of any **Nintendo Switch 2** version. And **PC**? There is no **PC release date**, Rockstar has not announced a PC version at all. [Take-Two Interactive](https://www.take2games.com/) CEO **Strauss Zelnick** has said in an interview that Rockstar always starts with console because it wants to serve its core audience first. That is a company choice, not a technical thing, and **GTA V** also came to PC much later than the console version. Some insiders say early 2027 but that is just talk from insiders and not something Rockstar has said, so do not plan your PC upgrade around it.

## GTA 6 Features at Launch

Now let me quickly tell you about the game itself, without going deep. It is set in **Leonida**, which is a fictional state inspired by Florida, and you are back in **Vice City** after a very long time. You play two characters, **Jason** and **Lucia**, who are a criminal couple in the Bonnie and Clyde way and Lucia is the first female lead in the main series. The map is said to be more than twice the size of GTA V but that is a reported thing so take it that way. Radio has **Bad Bunny** and **Lana Del Rey** as DJs and for the first time you can listen to podcasts on the phone of your characters. **Rockstar** is targeting **30 frames per second** on the launch consoles and the **PS5 Pro** gets some extra visual improvements. And it is **single-player only** at launch. A new **online mode** is expected but Bloomberg reported that it may not come until 2027 and Rockstar has not given any date, and **Take-Two** said it will keep supporting **GTA Online** meanwhile.
